2. Scarlet Wash: Operations & Business Strategy Plan

Business Strategy & Ops

Developed a comprehensive operations, expansion, and service design strategy for Scarlet Wash, an on-campus laundry service engineered to optimize time management and well-being for college students. The business model features subscription and pay-per-use plans, integrated university drop-off locations, eco-friendly washing practices, and structured employee engagement frameworks.

Key Highlights & Insights:
  • Designed a standardized SIPOC workflow and operational strategy targeting an 80% quarter-over-quarter client retention rate.
  • Formulated national franchising and international joint-venture expansion models, including targeted add-on services (same-day rush, stain care, seasonal storage) to maximize average order value.
